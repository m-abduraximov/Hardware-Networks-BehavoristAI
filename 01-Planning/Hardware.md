1.   What is Hardware?

Computer hardware is the physical elements that make up a computer system. But what is a difference between hardware and software? You can imagine this, architect ( software  ) that tells the company  ( computer)  what to do, while engineer ( hard drive ) actually makes it happen. Common hardware components consist of CPU, RAM, input and output devices.

2. Main computer parts
- Motherboard

The motherboard is the thing to which every other component is connected. Its purpose is to help these components communicate with each other. It's relatively cheap compared to other components, even though it is arguably the most important one. Motherboard is  the heart of the computer.

-  CPU

CPU (Central Processing Unit) is the brain of your computer. Everything that  happens in the computer needs to go through the CPU at some point. In general CPU carries out instructions and performs calculations like complex mathematical operations or reading through massive lists.

- Hard Drive

A hard drive is where all of your data is stored, so when a computer says that you are running out of space, it means that the hard drive is almost full. It is good for storing large amounts of data, but it is really slow at accessing it.
To avoid problems with transferring information between the hard drive and the CPU, we use RAM.

- RAM

RAM (Random Access Memory) has low storage capacity, but it compensates for this with fast access to information, making it useful for quickly communicating with the CPU.
One bad thing is that you can’t permanently store data in RAM, so every time you shut down your computer, the data stored in it gets wiped out. This is the reason you see a loading screen every time you reopen a video game. At that moment, the needed data is transferred from the hard drive to RAM so the CPU can access it much faster.

- SSD

An SSD (Solid State Drive) is basically a hard drive on steroids. They have the same goal, but an SSD is faster and more durable. It is a newer technology that can be used alongside a hard drive or on its own. Because of these advantages, it usually costs more money.

- GPU

To create a 3D world, a computer has to do a massive amount of work, but the hardest part is actually showing those worlds on your screen. This is where we use a GPU (Graphics Processing Unit), a dedicated processor designed to render graphics and show the right things on your screen.

- Input and Output Devices

Input devices are things that we use to give information to the computer. Basically they allow us to communicate with the computer and tell it what to do. Common examples of input devices are keyboard, mouse and microphone.

Output devices are things that the computer uses to show us the results of what it has done. For example a monitor shows us images, speakers produce sound and a printer can print documents. Basically input devices help us send information to the computer while output devices allow the computer to communicate back to us.

- Cooling System

Things like CPU or GPU can easily heat up and burn out or damage other parts of the computer. This is why we need a cooling system. At the moment we have two types and it is either air cooling or liquid cooling, each with its own pros and cons.
